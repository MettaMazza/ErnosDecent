## 1. The Unified Worldview

There is one mechanism. It has worn many faces.

The core thesis across all five books is that a single, four-century, debt-based money-creation mechanism — **the Devil** — operates through named human operators — **the Demons** — behind a set of institutional surfaces — **the Masks**. The four (later five) masks present themselves as separate domains (markets, the state, the church, consensus-knowledge), but they share the same operators, the same money flows, and the same coordination architecture. They are not four systems. They are one system showing four faces.

**The Devil = the mechanism.** Operationally: debt-based money creation by private banks at compound interest, with no routine debt cancellation. The lineage is dated and continuous — Amsterdam 1602/1609 (VOC joint-stock company + Wisselbank) → Bank of England 1694 (William Paterson; the Bank "hath benefit of interest on all moneys which it creates out of nothing") → US First Bank → Federal Reserve 1913 → Nixon shock 1971 → petrodollar → 2008/2020 bailouts. Because principal is created at loan-time but the interest to service it is never created, the system can only be sustained by issuing new debt: it must expand perpetually or collapse. "It either expands or it collapses. It does not rest." In the deeper hermetic ontology she gives in interview (relayed in *Behind the Creator*), the Devil is **corrupted Ego** — the root evil itself.

**The Demons = the operators.** The named senior people and named captured institutions that run the masks. They are treated as interchangeable structural nodes, not villains: "a different set of operators, similarly positioned, would produce a similar set of operational behaviours." Individual intentions are declared irrelevant to the consequence. In the deeper ontology: the corrupted ego made flesh in the person who does the harm.

**The Masks = the public-facing surfaces.** Each mask presents as one thing while operating as another. Five masks total: financial (God/market), political (the State), religious (the Church), consensus/epistemic (the Knowing), and a fifth **Unity** mask (global-cooperation and governance bodies) whose function is to hide the integration of the other four. The structural argument for needing four/five masks: no single mask could carry public consent to four centuries of continuous extraction, so legitimacy is distributed across sectors that *present as independent*.

**Why it is one system, not four.** The singular-root chain: concentrated wealth requires political protection, which requires legitimacy machinery, which requires financial funding — and senior figures' careers run continuously across all four masks. Integration is documented through (1) senior-figure overlap across masks, (2) funding-flow integration across mask boundaries, (3) a year-round coordination architecture (Davos, Bilderberg, Trilateral, CFR, BIS, G30), (4) cross-mask policy influence, (5) cross-sustaining presentation. The falsifiability test she sets: the theorem fails if any one mask can be shown to operate independently of the other three — at operator, flow, vehicle, and revolving-door level — over 1602–2026. She states the record shows you cannot.

**Crucial scope discipline.** This is explicitly *not* a claim of a secret directorate or metaphysical unity. "The chapter does not posit a single coordinator, a secret directorate, or a metaphysical unity behind the masks." The claim is operational and structural: there is enough documented integration that "one captured system" is the more accurate reading than "four independent systems."

## 2. The Four / Five Masks

### Mask I — The Financial System ("God") · Vol. 1

**What it is.** Capital itself — the debt-money mechanism — worn as an impersonal god everyone obeys but no one sees. "There is a god in the room." Its vocabulary (liquidity, velocity, quantitative easing, securitisation) is "a vestment": built so the population can't follow what is done to it.

**Core argument.** The system is mathematically impossible to sustain and recognisably so to anyone who can do the arithmetic — which is why access to the arithmetic is suppressed. Built in three movements: philosophical (Aristotle's *paraphysin* / usury against nature; Kant; Rawls; Graeber's jubilee history), logical (the "lock" at individual, national, and international levels — the BIS as the un-exitable apex), and mathematical (compound interest paradox; Soddy's virtual vs real wealth; Piketty's r > g; growth as life-support; the trap). "We are not failing the arithmetic. We are being prevented from doing it." Verdict: "This book is the arithmetic done."

**Key historical lineage.** Amsterdam 1609 (the four-element architecture: public bank, joint-stock corporation, securities market, merchant-oligarchy-as-state) → London transplant via the Glorious Revolution → **Bank of England 1694** (national debt as a permanent, war-favouring instrument) → Rothschild five-city network → American argument (Hamilton vs Jefferson; Jackson's veto; the greenback experiment) → Jekyll Island 1910 → Federal Reserve 1913 → Bretton Woods 1944 → Nixon 1971 → the secret 1974 petrodollar deal → 2008.

**The thing-as-lived vs the captured institution.** Money as a genuine social technology — "money is not a thing, it is a relationship" — versus the captured mechanism of private money creation at compound interest. The book holds up sovereign money (Lincoln's greenbacks, the Chicago Plan, the IMF Kumhof-Benes paper) as proof "the private intermediary is a choice, not a necessity."

### Mask II — The State (the Political Class) · Vol. 2

**What it is.** Government as a lit stage of titled performers. "There is a stage in the room... This is the performance." The people on stage — with titles, microphones, prepared positions — do not own the machine; they are paid to operate it. Elections, parties, and the left-right axis are "the show the population watches instead of looking past the stage to the machine."

**Core argument.** The political-class *function* — managing the relationship between the population that produces value and the creditor class that extracts it — is universal across 4,000 years and ~20 civilisations. It can be run for the population (periodic debt cancellation — Mesopotamian *amargi* / *andurarum* / *misharum*) or for the creditor class (institutional permanence of debt). After roughly 1400 BCE the cancellations stopped; everything since has been the second mode. The modern parliamentary state is the latest staffing of an arrangement continuously operated for three hundred years. War is named as one of the machine's two normal modes — an extraction operation, not a foreign-policy accident.

**Key historical lineage.** **Bank of England 1694 → Walpole.** In April 1721 Robert Walpole became Britain's first de facto prime minister; the Bank, chartered in 1694 to finance William III's wars, by then held sovereign debt exceeding annual tax revenue. Walpole (a South Sea Company shareholder) "took office to manage the crisis on behalf of the bondholders"; the directors he could not protect were prosecuted, the figures he was protecting were not. His arrangement — "a political class governing on behalf of, and at the discretion of, the financial class to which the state was indebted — became the operating template of every modern parliamentary government." Deeper lineage: Walpole → Jakob Fugger → Crassus. Hamilton consciously imported the post-1694 settlement into the United States.

**The thing-as-lived vs the captured institution.** Genuine self-government and democratic deliberation versus the electoral/partisan "performance," where voting inside the captured party system is participation in the show, not control of the machine.

### Mask III — The Church (the Religious Apparatus) · Vol. 3

**What it is.** The captured religious-institutional infrastructure that takes the believer's belief, labour, financial flow, political legitimation, and consent, and routes them into the four-century mechanism. "There is a demon in the church. It smiles while you pray."

Two categories are distinguished: captured-by-state apparatuses (the proselytising majorities) vs targeted-by-state communities (the persecuted minorities the apparatuses crushed). Cross-tradition reactionary cooperation is named "the ecumenism of hate."

**Key historical lineage.** Constantine → medieval consolidation (Donation of Constantine forgery, Gregorian Reform, Crusades, Inquisition) → late-medieval indulgence trade financed by Fugger/Medici → contemporary apparatuses (the Vatican/IOR, the American evangelical-political network, the Kirill–Putin Moscow Patriarchate, Saudi petro-Islam from the 1744 Pact of Diriyah, Iranian *velayat-e faqih*, the Israeli religious-political state).

### Mask IV — Consensus / The Knowing (the Epistemic Apparatus) · Vol. 4

**What it is.** The captured consensus-knowledge apparatus — press, academy, peer-review, journals, philanthropy, regulation, public health, climate policy, think-tanks, fact-checking, algorithmic curation, and AI. "There is a demon in the consensus. It wears the lab coat as a vestment."

"Underneath the capture, the procedure remains."

## 6. The Voice Guide

How Maria writes, made actionable so a writer can match it. Two registers coexist: the **liturgical/documentary** register of the books (flat, checkable, incantatory) and the **living/spoken** register from the interview (Glaswegian, profane, mystic). Lean on the first for argument, the second for human texture.

**Declarative cold opens.** Begin a chapter or section with a short, flat sentence that drops the reader in. "There is a god in the room." / "There is a stage in the room." / "There is a demon in the church." / "There is a demon in the consensus." / "Capital is not the first mask. It is the current one."

**Triadic anaphora and escalation.** Stack parallel clauses, usually in threes, building to a hammer. "It transferred wealth from the public to its owners." / "The outcome is the function. The suffering is the yield." / "The roots are traceable. The names are named. The dates are dated." / "The believer is not the demon. The demon is not the faith. The faith is not the apparatus."

**Bimodal sentence length — hammer + long.** Long, clause-laden, evidence-dense sentences (60–90 words, packed with dates, sums, proper nouns) alternate with a curt 3–6 word verdict. The long sentence builds the case; the short one delivers it. "He stayed for twenty-one years." / "The arrangement is old. The actors are paid."

**Second-person address and mild imperatives.** Trust the reader to draw the conclusion. "You are going to look inside it." / "Hold the word in your hand." / "Audit it." / "The reader can."

**Naming as the central act.** The entire rhetorical engine is the contrast between what can and cannot be named. Replace the historian's passive voice ("the Bank of England was established") with active naming, "because the active voice would require naming the men who did it." "This book names it." / "The book names them." / "The demons are not metaphor."

**Consistent, physical metaphor — never decorative.** The mask, the vestment, the stage light, the fence, the granary, the trap, the clock, the collateral, the smile, the strings, the puppeteer. The mask moves across history; the face behind it does not.

**Etymology as a move.** *Con-spirare* = "to breathe together"; *paraphysin* = against nature; *tokos* = offspring; *amargi* = "return to mother," the first written word for freedom.

**Discipline that cuts both ways.** Debunk popular memes (the Titanic/Fed legend, a Rothschild pigeon story) *inside* chapters whose main thrust is to confirm coordination — establishing credibility for the claims she does make. "Where conspiracy is documented, the book names it. Where a popular conspiracy story collapses under primary-source examination, the book says so."

**Banned hedging vocabulary.** A stated rule against the "reward-hack vocabulary" of commercial AI: *substantive, substantially, fundamentally, essentially, effectively, ultimately, importantly, notably, clearly, obviously, indeed, of course, arguably.* No adjectival inflation; figures exact "where the record supports exactness." Prose names rather than qualifies.

**Compression as a virtue.** "The chapters are not less dense because they are shorter; they are shorter because they are denser."

**Two-beat aphoristic closes.** End on a sermon-like pair. "The shepherding has lasted four hundred years. / It does not have to last another forty." / "The remaining work is the work of the reader. / The book ends here." / "The volume is done."
