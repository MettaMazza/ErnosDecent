- **Register:** dense, declarative, prosecutorial-academic. Reads like an indictment crossed with a world-history textbook. Extremely high fact-density per line (lines run 300–600 words; the file is unusually heavy per line).

The book repeatedly distinguishes "the political-class point" from "the legal question."
- **Tone toward the captured press:** contemptuous, naming outlets and individual columnists as "the fence."
- **No flattery, no hedging of the central thesis; heavy reliance on named sources** (ProPublica, NYT investigations, Chilcot, court records, academic monographs cited inline — Maddison, Irfan Habib, Mike Davis, Amartya Sen, Earl Hamilton, Leslie Peirce).
