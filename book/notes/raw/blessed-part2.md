## 2. Core Arguments in the Range

1. **The "captured-state apparatus" frame, applied symmetrically across every tradition.** Each tradition has a working institutional apparatus that has fused with state/economic/political power — the Vatican (sovereign state), American evangelicalism (distributed network), Moscow Patriarchate (church-state symphony), Saudi (Al ash-Sheikh/Al Saud pact), Iran (velayat-e faqih clerical supremacy), Israel (ethno-religious state). Each chapter insists the analytic is applied "without exceptionalism for any tradition."
2. **Every apparatus exhibits the same institutional contradictions:** claims to moral authority contradicted by abuse cover-up; claims to financial integrity contradicted by recurrent scandal; claims to neutrality/independence contradicted by state alignment.
3. **Cross-tradition reactionary cooperation = "the ecumenism of hate."** Theologically incompatible actors (Catholic, Evangelical, Russian Orthodox, OIC, Hindu nationalist) cooperate on shared opposition to LGBTQ rights, reproductive rights, "gender ideology," and secular human-rights frameworks — cooperation persisting even across hard geopolitical rupture (Russia-West post-2022).
4. **Religious framing as "structuring discourse" in war** — not the cause of wars, not mere decoration, but "what these wars wore": the language of declaration ("Al-Aqsa Flood," "Operation Gideon's Chariots," Kirill's "Holy War"), mobilisation banner, sanctification of atrocity, and export vehicle.
5. **The unifying thesis (Ch.32–34): one mechanism, many masks.** A single "four-century debt-based money-creation mechanism" (proven, the book says, in Volume One Ch.7) requires ever-expanding "extractive surface." The captured religious apparatus is the THIRD operational layer (after finance, Vol.1; politics, Vol.2). It extracts through four channels: **direct financial flows, labour, political legitimation, consent.**
6. **The architectural counter-claim (the moral core): the believer is not the demon.** "The believer is not the demon. The demon is not the faith. The faith is not the apparatus." The apparatus depends on believers conflating the three. The book's stated job is documentation, not direction — "Direction belongs to the believer."
