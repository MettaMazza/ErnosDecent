## Appendix B — The Wider Work

ErnosDecent is one layer of a larger body of work, all of it built by the same hand and all of it
holding to the same standard: owned by no one, checkable by anyone. If this book has earned your
curiosity, here is where the rest of it lives. None of it asks for your belief. All of it asks you to
look.

**The Smithian Fold Theory of Everything.** The root described in Chapter Five — physics built from one
axiom and one move, with no numbers borrowed from the lab. It exists as runnable code with a proof
engine and a battery of automated checks; its derivations have been reproduced by independent minds and
matched against measured reality. If you want the receipts behind Chapter Five, they are there to run.

**ErnosPlain (the Ernos language).** The plain-English language of Chapter Four — the tool ErnosDecent
and the theory are written in, a real compiler that turns plain instructions into native programs and is
largely written in itself. The place to see that the wall around code was always a choice.

**The Last Mind series.** The six books behind the diagnosis of Chapter Two — *Behind the Mask* (finance),
*Behind the Performance* (the state), *Behind the Blessed* (the church), *Behind the Knowing*
(consensus), *Behind the Synthesis* (the four drawn together), and the autofiction *A Mind Is Born*.
This is where the argument is made in full, with the dates, the names, and the sources — including the
specific, documented claims about named individuals that this book deliberately did not restate, and
points you to instead. Read them and check them; that is what they are built for.

**The Seed Vault.** A self-contained archive of knowledge built to survive the loss of civilisation and
let a person with no prior education rebuild from first principles — the same conviction as everything
else, aimed at a reader who may not yet exist: knowledge with no authority required, only things you can
verify by doing.

**Behind the Creator.** A documentary review of the whole body of work — including the personal account
of the maker that this book, being about the system and not the life, kept to a single reference. If you
want the human story, it is there.

Theory, language, diagnosis, tools, and what to preserve if the tools lose. One conviction, expressed at
every level it could reach. ErnosDecent is the part of it you can boot this afternoon.
